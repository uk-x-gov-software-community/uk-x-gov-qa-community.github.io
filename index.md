## Our Vision
To establish a vibrant and cohesive community of QAT professionals who are empowered to drive innovation, excellence, and continuous improvement in quality assurance and testing practices across the UK Civil Service.

Our vision helps us fullfil our  [Objectives](/objectives).

## How are we doing this
* Engaging with the community on [UK Government Digital Slack](https://ukgovernmentdigital.slack.com/) - in the #qa-test channel 
*	Developing our GitHub repo
*	Looking to run more themed events 
*	Hosting virtual and in person Quality Assurance and Testing meetups
*	We have a small facilitation group that is doing some of the initial setup
